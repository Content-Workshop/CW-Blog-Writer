# Brief

**Working Title:** Content Strategy Decides What Your Website Becomes
**Target Persona:** In-house marketing leader (CMO / VP Marketing) who owns a website rebuild and is negotiating scope, timeline, and budget with a design or dev partner
**Core Keyword:** website content strategy
**Subtopic Keyword:** content-first web design
**Search Intent:** Strategic / commercial-informational — the reader is actively scoping or mid-project and wants to know where content strategy sits in the build sequence and what it changes
**Reader Problem:** The build is priced and scheduled around design deliverables. Content arrives last, so the site gets structured before anyone decides what it has to say, and the launch date slips on copy nobody owns.
**Content Angle:** Content strategy is not a feeder deliverable to the build. It determines the build: the sitemap, the templates, the page count, and therefore the quote. Argued through the sequence of decisions, not through benefits.
**Internal Links:** One pillar page + two internal articles, marked inline as `[PILLAR: ...]` and `[INTERNAL: ...]` with recommended anchor text (URLs pending from @data)
**CTA Direction:** Concludes the argument — offer the strategy layer (audit, message hierarchy, content models) as the thing that goes in front of the design partner's estimate

## Keyword placement — one call worth your 10 seconds

The main instructions say to use the exact-match phrase **once** and also to place it in the title, intro, an H2, body, and CTA. Those can't both be satisfied literally, so I placed the single exact match in the **intro, word ~73** (the rule names "first ~100 words" explicitly), and used close variants elsewhere. The H1 therefore does not carry the exact phrase.

If you'd rather have it in the H1 for SEO weight, swap the title to **"Website Content Strategy Decides What Gets Built"** and change the intro sentence to "Content strategy for a rebuild inverts that order..." That keeps the count at one. Your call — both are defensible, and I'd lean toward the H1 version if this piece is meant to rank rather than to be shared.
