- **Bundle promise:** move from isolated, single-use AI tasks to orchestrating connected marketing systems that blend human creativity, AI reasoning, and workflow automation, making your team the AI epicenter of your company.

## Course content

### AI Marketing Operator (Part 1)
1. **The AI Mindset & The "Human-AI-Human Sandwich"** (canonical name; course copy says "Human Sandwich"): AI as the "velocity filling" between original strategy and final human review.
2. **Building Your AI Strategy:** map workflows to find tedious bottlenecks where AI can safely save hours.
3. **The Modern Marketing AI Toolkit:** "Connectivity over Consolidation"; match specific tools to the tasks they excel at.
4. **Prompt Engineering as System Logic:** 8-part structural framework (boundaries, context, success criteria).

### AI Marketing Architect (Part 2)
5. **Building Custom AI Co-workers:** permanent, task-specific agents via the "4 Ps" framework, loaded with brand guidelines and negative constraints.
6. **Automation vs. AI & Workflow Mapping:** "Automation moves data; AI makes meaning." 5-stage architecture; handoff logic between platforms and agents.
7. **Implementation, Management, & Ethics:** Minimum Viable Output (MVO), agent maintenance, mandatory "Authenticity Filter."
